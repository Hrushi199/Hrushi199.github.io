CNN inference engine ASIC, RTL to GDSII

Apr – May 2026SystemVerilogTclCadence GenusCadence InnovusTeam of 6

An energy-efficient CNN accelerator based on the memory-sharing and data-reuse architecture of Islam et al. (IEEE TCAS-I, 2024), taken through the complete physical design flow on the gsclib045 45nm library.

  • 864 processing elements in a 9 x 96 array with a Q8.8 fixed-point datapath, plus controller and classification units.
  • Cut dynamic power by 61.6% with RTL clock gating, operand isolation, bus gating, Gray-coded FSMs, 18,461 tool-inserted clock gates and multi-Vt mapping.
  • Closed timing with MMMC analysis at SS 1.0V/125°C (setup) and FF 1.2V/−40°C (hold), with OCV and CPPR.

Mixed-precision NVFP4 / BF16 RISC-V inference engine

Jun – Jul 2026SystemVerilogPythonPyTorchVivadoCadence Genus

A RISC-V SoC that runs neural network layers in NVIDIA's 4-bit NVFP4 format where the accuracy allows it, and in BF16 where it does not. It covers the whole path from software analysis to synthesizable RTL.

  • Designed NVFP4 and BF16 dot-product units and integrated them into the open-source CV32E40P core, extending its decoder with 7 instructions under the Custom-0 opcode.
  • Wrote Python generators for a 188-instruction RISC-V test program and its expected results; the full SoC passes all 14 self-checking tests (7 NVFP4, 7 BF16) in Vivado.
  • Built a PyTorch layer-sensitivity analysis and scheduler for 6 networks that keeps accuracy within 1% of FP32.
  • Synthesized on 45nm GPDK045 with Cadence Genus at 200 MHz, reporting area, power and timing.

Low-power retentive TSPC flip-flop

Mar – Apr 2026Cadence VirtuosoSpectre45nm CMOS

An implementation of the redundant-precharge-free TSPC flip-flop from You et al. (IEEE TVLSI, 2021). An input-aware precharge transistor stops internal nodes from toggling when the data input does not change.

  • Designed schematics and layout for the proposed flip-flop and 7 more variants: set, reset, scan, SEU-tolerant, and TGFF, ACFF and S2CFF baselines.
  • Characterized setup time, hold time and power for every variant, with contention-free operation from 1.2V down to below 0.3V.
